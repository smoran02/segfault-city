Segfault City

An open city where every job is a few lines of C++. Read the code, decide what it does, then drive to the answer.

  • Pairs, one device per pair. A laptop shows the code best. One of you drives, one reads the code, and you swap on every new mission.
  • Take a mission by driving to an orange ring and pressing Space. Any order works.
  • Brains only. No compilers, no AI. Trace it like the exam.
  • Wrong pick: a compiler warning. Three on one job is a segmentation fault and a 12-second restart.
  • Each job pays up to $300. A wrong pick or a hint takes $100 off. A segfault takes $200 off. Hints unlock after a minute.
  • Every job ends with a delivery across town. A fast drive that stays clear of traffic adds a tip of up to $100. A wrong pick or a hint on the job halves the tip.
  • Spend your cash at the Mod Shop next to the fountain: engine, tires, nitro, a louder horn.
  • Sound is on. H honks, and traffic gets out of your way. M mutes.
  • Back from last class? Your progress is saved on this device.
